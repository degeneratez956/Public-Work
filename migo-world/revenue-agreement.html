SECTION 3

Duration & Termination

This agreement begins on the date signed and continues indefinitely while the website is active. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice (text or email acceptable).

If Artist Terminates — Buyout Formula
(Avg Monthly Gross × 2%) × 24 months
Site avg $5,000/mo gross$100/mo × 24 = $2,400
Site avg $10,000/mo gross$200/mo × 24 = $4,800
Site avg $40,000/mo gross$800/mo × 24 = $19,200
Minimum buyout$500
Maximum buyout (cap)$25,000

Average calculated from the 3 most recent full calendar months at time of termination notice.

If Web Designer terminates: Artist keeps the website. Revenue share ends. No buyout required.

If website goes inactive: Revenue share pauses. Resumes automatically if website reactivates.
